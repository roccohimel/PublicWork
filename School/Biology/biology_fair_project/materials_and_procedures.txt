Materials

- 1 apple (or several of the same kind)
- Knife and cutting board
- 5 cups or bowls
- Water, lemon juice, saltwater, vinegar, milk
- Measuring cup
- Paper towels
- Plates or tray
- Labels or marker
- Timer
- Camera
- Ruler
- Data table and pencil

Procedures

1. Label 5 cups: water, lemon juice, saltwater, vinegar, milk.
2. Pour the same amount of each liquid into its cup.
3. Cut the apple into equal slices, one per liquid.
4. Dip each slice in its liquid for 30 seconds.
5. Take the slices out, pat them dry, and set them on a labeled plate.
6. Start the timer and take a photo of each slice.
7. Every 15 minutes, record the browning on a 1-5 scale and note the color and texture.
8. Repeat for 1 to 2 hours and take a photo at each check.
9. Compare the results to see which liquid slowed browning the most.
