FINAL DATA LOG SHEET
Experiment: Effects of Different Liquids on Apple Browning

Purpose: To determine which liquid prevents apple slices from browning the most over 90 minutes.

Independent Variable: Type of liquid used.
Dependent Variable: Amount of apple browning.
Controlled Variables: Apple slice size, amount of liquid, dipping time (30 seconds), observation intervals, and environmental conditions.

1. QUANTITATIVE DATA

Browning scale: 1 = no browning; 5 = severe browning.

Time (min) | Water | Lemon | Saltwater | Vinegar | Milk
-----------|-------|-------|-----------|---------|-----
0          | 1     | 1     | 1         | 1       | 1
15         | 1     | 1     | 1.5       | 1.5     | 1
30         | 1     | 1     | 1.5       | 2       | 1
45         | 1     | 1     | 2         | 2.5     | 1
60         | 1     | 1     | 2         | 3       | 1
75         | 1     | 1     | 2.5       | 3.5     | 1
90         | 1     | 1     | 2.5       | 4       | 1

2. QUALITATIVE DATA

Before the experiment, all apple slices were bright yellow and smooth.

Liquid       | Color After 90 Minutes | Texture After 90 Minutes
-------------|------------------------|-------------------------
Water        | Same                   | Rough
Lemon Juice  | Lime-green             | Same (smooth)
Saltwater    | Slight browning        | Soggy
Vinegar      | Heavy browning         | Soggy
Milk         | White                  | Rough

3. FINAL STATISTICAL DATA

Statistics use all seven measurements for each liquid.

Liquid       | Mean | Median | Mode          | Range | Change (0-90 min)
-------------|------|--------|---------------|-------|------------------
Water        | 1.00 | 1      | 1             | 0     | 0
Lemon Juice  | 1.00 | 1      | 1             | 0     | 0
Saltwater    | 1.86 | 2      | 1.5, 2, 2.5   | 1.5   | +1.5
Vinegar      | 2.50 | 2.5    | No mode       | 3     | +3
Milk         | 1.00 | 1      | 1             | 0     | 0

Mean = Sum of all measurements / Number of measurements
Median = Middle measurement when arranged in order
Mode = Most frequently occurring measurement
Range = Highest measurement - Lowest measurement
Change = Final measurement - Initial measurement

Example using vinegar:
Mean = (1 + 1.5 + 2 + 2.5 + 3 + 3.5 + 4) / 7 = 2.5
Median = 2.5
Mode = None
Range = 4 - 1 = 3
Change = 4 - 1 = 3

5. OBSERVATIONS AND RESULTS

During the experiment, water, lemon juice, and milk
prevented visible browning, maintaining a score of 1. Saltwater
showed slight browning, reaching aa score of 2.5. Vinegar caused
the most browning, reaching a score of 4. The apples also showed
texture changes, with saltwater and vinegar making the slices soggy.
Lemon juice maintained the original smooth texture but changed the
apple's color to slightly lime-green.
