Applications

This experiment can be useful in everyday life, especially when preparing apples for school lunches, snacks, or fruit salads.
If someone cuts up an apple ahead of time, they probably want it to stay fresh-looking instead of turning brown. Based on my
results, lemon juice would be a good choice because the apple slice did not brown during the 90 minutes and stayed smooth.
Water and milk also prevented visible browning, but those slices became rough. Saltwater caused some browning and made the
apple soggy, while vinegar had the most browning.

These results could also help people who prepare fruit at restaurants or cafeterias. Lemon juice seemed to work best overall
in my experiment because it kept the apple from browning without making its texture worse. I would need to test the slices 
for longer to see whether the results stay the same after several hours.
