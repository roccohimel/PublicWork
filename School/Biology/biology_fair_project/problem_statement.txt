Problem Statement:
Does the type of liquid an apple slice is dipped in affect how quickly and severely it turns brown?

Quantitative Data:
- Time (minutes/hours) for browning to begin
- Percentage (%) or area (cm^2) of apple slice surface that turns brown
- Browning rate measured at set intervals (example: every 15 min)
- Numerical browning scale ratings (example: 1-5) if using a rubric

Qualitative Data:
- Type of liquid used (water, lemon juice, saltwater, vinegar, milk, ... - independent variable)
- Color of the apple slice (pale yellow, light brown, dark brown, black)
- Texture/appearance changes (mushy, dry, spotted)
- Photos comparing slices side by side
